Settlor

Web Developer, December 2021 to now

Settlor is a title insurance and escrow company that runs on software it builds itself: an internal operations platform that covers title, closing, escrow accounting and e-recording, white-label portals for lenders and agents, and a public API. I've worked on that software for nearly five years.

My title is Web Developer, and the work covers the whole stack: Flask and FastAPI services, PostgreSQL, Vue screens and Docker images. I've done more every year, and 2026 is my biggest year by far.

Here's the stack. Parts of it are mid-move: Python to 3.14, SQLAlchemy to 2.0, and the frontend from Vue 2 and webpack to Vue 3 and Vite.

Remote print service

2023 to 2026. I built it end to end, on my own.

The problem

Print jobs come from the cloud app, and the printers are at office sites.

TODO(zach): why the service had to run on site.

What I did

I wrote a Windows service that gets installed at office sites. It receives print jobs from the cloud app and drives the local printers over IPP. It has:

  • A job queue with retry.
  • Recovery when a worker thread dies.
  • A web admin page.
  • A signed installer, built with PyInstaller and Inno Setup.
